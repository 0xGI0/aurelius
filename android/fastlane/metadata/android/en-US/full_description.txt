Three Stoic classics in one app: Marcus Aurelius' "Meditations" (486 sections), Epictetus' "Enchiridion" (53 chapters) and Seneca's "On the Shortness of Life" (20 chapters) — each in German, English and the original (Ancient Greek or Latin).

Features:

• Author switch with portraits and a comparison of the three Stoics
• A random thought at the press of a button — repetition-free shuffle
• Nine topic filters: death, anger, grief, fear, family, possessions, equanimity, duty, nature
• All twelve books to browse and read
• Favorites — local, or synced across devices with a free account
• AI explanation of individual passages (optional, needs internet)
• Bilingual interface (German/English), light and dark theme

The app works fully offline — only account sync and AI explanations need a connection. No ads, no tracking.
